# Marketing Mix Model

A marketing mix model uses statistical analysis—often regression across marketing and sales data—to estimate how different marketing activities are associated with commercial outcomes.

This notebook was an early exercise in understanding how quantitative methods can support marketing investment decisions.

## Why it remains on my profile

The project reflects a long-standing interest in connecting marketing decisions with data. I keep it public as part of the progression from foundational analysis to my current product marketing and AI operating-system work.

## Limitations

- The repository is retained as learning history.
- Results should not be treated as production recommendations without data-quality, model-validation and causal-inference checks.
